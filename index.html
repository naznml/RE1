Unit 1 · Subunit 1 · Reading 1

The Importance of Friendship

Достықтың маңызы Важность дружбы

Бұл reading мәтіні достықтың адам өміріндегі рөлі, эмоционалдық қолдау, сенім, коммуникация және шынайы қарым-қатынас туралы. Мәтіннен нақты ақпаратты табу, негізгі ойды анықтау және себеп-салдар байланысын түсіну қажет.

Этот reading text о роли дружбы в жизни человека, эмоциональной поддержке, доверии, коммуникации и настоящих отношениях. Нужно находить specific information, понимать main idea и cause-effect relationships.

Мәтін Текст

B1 IELTS Reading Practice – Level B1

Topic: The Importance of Friendship

Friendship is one of the most valuable relationships in a person's life. A true friend is someone who supports you during difficult times and celebrates with you during happy moments. Unlike family, friends are people we choose. This makes friendship special, because it is based on trust, respect, and shared experiences.

Many studies show that people with strong friendships tend to be happier and healthier. Good friends can reduce stress, improve self-confidence, and even help you live longer. When you feel sad or lonely, having someone to talk to can make a big difference. In fact, emotional support from friends is as important as physical health.

However, building and maintaining strong friendships is not always easy. Friends may have different opinions, interests, or life goals. Misunderstandings and arguments can happen. That’s why communication is key. Listening, being honest, and showing empathy can help strengthen any relationship.

Today, technology also plays a big role in friendship. Social media and messaging apps allow people to stay in touch, even when they live far apart. While online communication is helpful, it cannot fully replace face-to-face time. Spending time together, sharing activities, or simply having a real conversation are all important parts of friendship.

In short, friendship adds joy and meaning to life. It is not about having many friends, but having a few real ones who truly care.

Reading 1 · Questions

1. Why is friendship described as special in the passage?

B дұрыс. Мәтінде friends are people we choose деп жазылған. Сондықтан friendship special, себебі ол trust, respect және shared experiences негізінде құрылады.
B правильно. В тексте сказано, что friends are people we choose, and friendship is based on trust, respect and shared experiences.

2. According to the passage, what can strong friendships do for people?

D дұрыс. Екінші абзацта good friends can reduce stress, improve self-confidence деп нақты айтылған.
D правильно. Во втором абзаце сказано: good friends can reduce stress and improve self-confidence.

3. What does the writer suggest about emotional support from friends?

A дұрыс. Мәтінде emotional support from friends is as important as physical health деп берілген.
A правильно. В тексте прямо сказано: emotional support from friends is as important as physical health.

4. Why can friendships sometimes become difficult?

C дұрыс. Үшінші абзацта friends may have different opinions, interests, or life goals деп жазылған. Бұл misunderstandings and arguments тудыруы мүмкін.
C правильно. В третьем абзаце сказано, что friends may have different opinions, interests, or life goals.

5. What is presented as the key to strengthening a friendship?

E дұрыс. Мәтінде communication is key деп айтылған, кейін listening, being honest, and showing empathy relationship-ті күшейтеді деп түсіндіріледі.
E правильно. В тексте сказано: communication is key, and listening, being honest and showing empathy can strengthen relationships.

6. What does the passage say about online communication?

B дұрыс. Social media and messaging apps адамдарға stay in touch көмектеседі, бірақ online communication face-to-face time-ды толық алмастыра алмайды.
B правильно. Online communication helps people stay in touch, but cannot fully replace face-to-face time.

7. Which activity is mentioned as an important part of friendship?

D дұрыс. Төртінші абзацта spending time together, sharing activities, having a real conversation friendship-тің маңызды бөлігі деп айтылған.
D правильно. Spending time together, sharing activities and having a real conversation are important parts of friendship.

8. What is the main message of the passage?

A дұрыс. Соңғы абзацта friendship көп дос болу емес, truly care ететін few real friends болу маңызды деп қорытынды жасалады.
A правильно. The final paragraph says friendship is not about having many friends, but having a few real ones who truly care.

Reading strategy

  • Алдымен мәтіннің тақырыбын және әр абзацтың негізгі ойын түсін.
  • Key words (кілт сөздер) сұрақтағы сөздермен дәл келмеуі мүмкін, сондықтан synonym (мағыналас сөз) ізде.
  • According to the passage (мәтін бойынша) сұрақтарында жауап мәтіннің ішінде нақты болуы керек.
  • Main message (негізгі ой) сұрағында бір сөйлемге емес, бүкіл passage (мәтін) мағынасына қара.
  • Сначала пойми тему текста и главную мысль каждого абзаца.
  • Key words (ключевые слова) могут не совпадать дословно, поэтому ищи synonym (синоним).
  • В вопросах According to the passage (согласно тексту) ответ должен подтверждаться текстом.
  • В main message (главная мысль) смотри не на одно предложение, а на весь passage (текст).
Unit 1 · Subunit 1 · Reading 2

Living on Your Own

Жеке өмір сүру тәжірибесі Опыт самостоятельной жизни

Бұл reading мәтіні өз алдына бөлек тұру, жеке кеңістік, отбасы құндылықтары, қаржылық жауапкершілік және күнделікті тұрмыс міндеттері туралы. Мәтінде автордың Гонконгтағы жеке өмір сүру тәжірибесі сипатталады.

Этот reading text о самостоятельной жизни, личном пространстве, семейных ценностях, финансовой ответственности и бытовых обязанностях. В тексте описывается личный опыт автора, который решил жить отдельно в Гонконге.

Мәтін Текст

B1 Reading Practice · Personal Space and Independent Living

Topic: Living on Your Own

I want to talk about my experience of moving out and living on my own. Moving out to live independently is not very common in Hong Kong. Many people still choose to live with their parents even when they have a decent job and are already in their thirties. Many of them stay with their parents until they get married.

There are several reasons for this. First, rent in Hong Kong is expensive. Many people would rather save money to buy an apartment than rent one. Second, according to traditional Chinese family values, parents are the people who brought you up. When you become an adult, you should also take good care of your parents and spend more time with them. Of course, some people are still dependent on their parents for food, clothing, household chores and other daily needs.

When I tell people about my idea of moving out, many people ask, “What do your parents say?” They assume that parents would not like their children to leave home. I do not know exactly how my parents feel about it, but I can say that they support and respect my decision. They think it is good for me to try living on my own because then I will understand whether it is best to stay with my family or not.

The most important reason for independent living is to save travelling time to work. It used to take me one hour and fifteen minutes to travel to work from my previous home. From my new apartment, it takes only thirty minutes, so I save forty-five minutes each way. I do not have to get up so early, and I also save two-thirds of my travelling cost.

In addition, I gain personal space and freedom by living independently. I make my own decisions all the time. I do not have to say whether I will go home for dinner, and I can invite my friends to come over and stay late. This freedom is one of the best parts of living on my own.

Reading 2 · Questions

1. What does the writer say about moving out in Hong Kong?

C дұрыс. Бірінші абзацта moving out to live independently is not very common in Hong Kong деп нақты жазылған.
C правильно. В первом абзаце сказано: moving out to live independently is not very common in Hong Kong.

2. Why do many adults continue living with their parents?

A дұрыс. Мәтінде екі негізгі себеп бар: rent expensive және traditional Chinese family values бойынша adult children parents-ке care жасау керек.
A правильно. Текст называет две причины: rent is expensive and traditional Chinese family values encourage adult children to care for parents.

3. What do traditional Chinese family values suggest?

E дұрыс. Екінші абзацта parents brought you up, сондықтан adult болғанда take good care of your parents and spend more time with them делінген.
E правильно. The passage says adults should take good care of their parents and spend more time with them.

4. What do many people assume when the writer talks about moving out?

B дұрыс. Мәтінде адамдар “What do your parents say?” деп сұрайды, себебі parents would not like their children to leave home деп assume етеді.
B правильно. People assume that parents would not like their children to leave home.

5. How do the writer’s parents react to the decision?

D дұрыс. Автор my parents support and respect my decision деп нақты айтады.
D правильно. The writer says: they support and respect my decision.

6. What is the most important reason for the writer’s independent living?

A дұрыс. Төртінші абзац “The most important reason for independent living is to save travelling time to work” деп басталады.
A правильно. The text says: The most important reason for independent living is to save travelling time to work.

7. How much travelling time does the writer save each way?

C дұрыс. Бұрын жол 1 hour and 15 minutes болған, жаңа apartment-тен 30 minutes. Сондықтан автор 45 minutes each way үнемдейді.
C правильно. It used to take 1 hour 15 minutes, now it takes 30 minutes, so the writer saves 45 minutes each way.

8. Which benefit of living independently is mentioned in the final paragraph of the shortened text?

E дұрыс. Қысқартылған мәтіннің соңғы абзацында автор personal space and freedom gain ететінін айтады.
E правильно. The final paragraph of the shortened text says the writer gains personal space and freedom.

Reading strategy

  • Автордың негізгі себебін табу үшін “The most important reason” деген тіркесті белгіле.
  • Numbers (сандар) кездессе, оларды салыстыр: 1 hour 15 minutes және 30 minutes.
  • Reason сұрақтарында because, first, second сияқты signal words (белгі сөздер) көмектеседі.
  • Benefit сұрақтарында personal space, freedom, travelling time сияқты нақты сөздерді ізде.
  • Чтобы найти главную причину автора, смотри на фразу “The most important reason”.
  • Если есть numbers (числа), сравнивай их: 1 hour 15 minutes и 30 minutes.
  • В reason questions помогают signal words (сигнальные слова): because, first, second.
  • В benefit questions ищи конкретные слова: personal space, freedom, travelling time.
