Бұл reading мәтіні достықтың адам өміріндегі рөлі, эмоционалдық қолдау, сенім, коммуникация және шынайы қарым-қатынас туралы. Мәтіннен нақты ақпаратты табу, негізгі ойды анықтау және себеп-салдар байланысын түсіну қажет.

Этот reading text о роли дружбы в жизни человека, эмоциональной поддержке, доверии, коммуникации и настоящих отношениях. Нужно находить specific information, понимать main idea и cause-effect relationships.

Мәтін Текст

B1 IELTS Reading Practice – Level B1

Topic: The Importance of Friendship

Friendship is one of the most valuable relationships in a person's life. A true friend is someone who supports you during difficult times and celebrates with you during happy moments. Unlike family, friends are people we choose. This makes friendship special, because it is based on trust, respect, and shared experiences.

Many studies show that people with strong friendships tend to be happier and healthier. Good friends can reduce stress, improve self-confidence, and even help you live longer. When you feel sad or lonely, having someone to talk to can make a big difference. In fact, emotional support from friends is as important as physical health.

However, building and maintaining strong friendships is not always easy. Friends may have different opinions, interests, or life goals. Misunderstandings and arguments can happen. That’s why communication is key. Listening, being honest, and showing empathy can help strengthen any relationship.

Today, technology also plays a big role in friendship. Social media and messaging apps allow people to stay in touch, even when they live far apart. While online communication is helpful, it cannot fully replace face-to-face time. Spending time together, sharing activities, or simply having a real conversation are all important parts of friendship.

In short, friendship adds joy and meaning to life. It is not about having many friends, but having a few real ones who truly care.

Reading 1 · Questions

4. What is a limitation of online communication in friendship?

6. What can happen because friends have different opinions or goals?

7. Which action can strengthen a friendship?

8. What is the main message of the passage?

Reading strategy

  • Алдымен мәтіннің тақырыбын және әр абзацтың негізгі ойын түсін.
  • Сұрақтағы key words мәтіндегі ұқсас сөздермен салыстыр.
  • “According to the passage” сұрақтарында жауап мәтіннің ішінде нақты болуы керек.
  • Main message сұрағында бір ғана сөйлемге емес, бүкіл мәтіннің жалпы ойына қара.
  • Сначала пойми тему текста и главную мысль каждого абзаца.
  • Сравни key words из вопроса с похожими словами в тексте.
  • В вопросах “according to the passage” ответ должен быть подтверждён текстом.
  • В main message смотри не на одно предложение, а на общий смысл всего passage.
